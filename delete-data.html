Flax Race: Delete Your Data

Flax Race ("the game") has no account and no sign-in. This page explains how to have the data that belongs to you removed. The Privacy Policy says what the game stores and sends.

Data on your phone

Your progress and settings are stored only on your phone. Uninstall the game, or open Android Settings > Apps > Flax Race > Storage and choose "Clear data", and they are gone. We cannot read or delete them from our side.

Usage data (Google Analytics)

If you allowed the usage data that the Privacy Policy describes, you can switch it off at any time in the Settings of the game under "Usage data": the game stops the reports at once and deletes the ones that were not sent yet. The reports that were already sent carry a random identifier that the game does not show and no name, so we cannot find the ones of a single person; Google Analytics deletes them by itself when their two months are over, in its next monthly clean-up. "Delete my data" in the Settings also deletes the reports that were not sent yet and gives the game a new identifier, and uninstalling the game removes the identifier from your phone.

Your purchase record at RevenueCat

If you bought something in the game, our service provider RevenueCat keeps a record of the purchase. To have it deleted:

  1. If you have a VIP subscription, cancel it first in Google Play under Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions. Deleting the record does not cancel a subscription.
  2. Write to ufukkaanguven@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Flax Race data".
  3. Put the Google Play order number of one of your purchases in the message. It looks like GPA.1234-5678-9012-34567 and is in your Google Play receipt e-mail and in your Google Play order history.
  4. We find the record by that number, delete it at RevenueCat and confirm by e-mail, normally within 30 days.

If you never bought anything, RevenueCat only has a random anonymous ID and technical information that we cannot link to you, so there is nothing to look up; uninstalling the game removes the ID from your phone.

Data that Google collects through the ads software

The optional rewarded video ads are delivered by Google AdMob. Google keeps the data its ads software collects under its own privacy policy. Use the controls in your Google account and, in the Android settings under Privacy > Ads, reset or delete your advertising ID.
